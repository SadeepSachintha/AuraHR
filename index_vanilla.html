Interactive Sales Guide

Showcase the reactive connection between Employee Mobile and HR Admin in three steps:

  1. Real-Time Clock In: On the Mobile Simulator (right), tap Clock In on the map. Instantly watch the Attendance Log update in the Admin Portal.
  2. Instant Leave Approval: Go to the mobile's Leave screen, apply for a Casual Leave. Instantly click Leaves on the Admin Sidebar to approve it. Notice the mobile app's status change in real time.
  3. Payroll Run: View the Payroll tab in the Admin panel. Calculate salary with custom allowances and observe Sri Lankan EPF (8%/12%) and ETF (3%) compliance in a professional payslip.
This entire mockup runs locally on localStorage. All calculations are real-time.
